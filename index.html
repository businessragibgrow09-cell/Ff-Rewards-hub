Complete Guide: How to Claim Rewards

Free Fire redeem codes are 12 or 16-character alphanumeric strings issued by Garena. These codes grant players free in-game items such as gun skins, room cards, bundles, and voucher cards without spending real diamonds.

How to Redeem Codes on Play Store & Official Site:

  1. Open your browser and navigate to the official Garena Rewards Redemption Portal (reward.ff.garena.com).
  2. Sign in using the social account connected to your game (Facebook, Google, Twitter, or VK). Guest accounts cannot claim rewards.
  3. Paste or manually enter the 12-character redeem code generated above into the empty field.
  4. Click on the Confirm button. A success dialogue box will confirm your redemption.
  5. Open Free Fire on your mobile device and check your In-game Mailbox to collect your items.
